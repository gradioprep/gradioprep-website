# English Curriculum Public-Source Register and Currency Protocol

**Version:** 1.6  
**Research checked:** 26 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Establish a reproducible, publicly usable source base for IB MYP Language and Literature, IB DP Language A: Language and Literature, Cambridge Lower Secondary English 0861, and Cambridge IGCSE First Language English 0500.  
**Scope note:** This is not an IB/Cambridge endorsement, nor does it make an independently designed unit an official syllabus map.

## 1. Decision in response to access constraints

The founder does not have school-account access to the IB Programme Resource Centre (PRC). We will therefore **not wait indefinitely for gated guides**. For MYP, we will build a clearly labelled, public-framework-aligned and evidence-informed Year 1 pathway using the official public subject overview, official public assessment information and carefully chosen public educational resources. For DP, use the public official course page and the publicly indexed 2026 examiner instructions where accessible, cross-checked against relevant specialist teaching sites. For Cambridge 0500, use the official public syllabus for exams 2027–2029 and its current specimen paper/mark-scheme set as the primary design source.

This change does **not** permit a claim that the latest detailed guide has been replicated or that current official Year 1 mapping has been verified. The 2014/15 detailed Language and Literature guide is now located as an indexed official IB resource and its historical Year 1 A–D descriptors can inform a clearly labelled legacy crosswalk. That edition is not proof of the newest/current descriptor wording. Keep current official mapping unverified until the applicable edition and continuity are confirmed. No official codes or wording from gated documents are to be guessed. For any course, official identifiers, assessment format and syllabus claims require traceable sources.

## 2. Core five-source set

These are the five cross-track core website/source families selected for relevance, traceability, currency and transparency—not a popularity ranking or a claim that they are the five best sites for every course. They do not form an exhaustive MYP-specific EdTech review. For MYP Year 1, use the additional dedicated landscape audit in docs/curriculum/IB_MYP_YEAR_1_RESOURCE_LANDSCAPE_REVIEW.md.

## 3. What counts as current enough to use

Every source or sample must pass the following checks before it drives a learning objective, question, rubric or route:

1. **Course match:** Exact programme, subject/course and language match; do not mix MYP with DP, Language A: Literature with Language A: Language and Literature, or 0500 with 0990/other English qualifications.
2. **Edition and session match:** Record publication/update date and, for exams, first-examination year or session. Use Cambridge 0500 2027–2029 specimens for candidates sitting the revised syllabus from 2027.
3. **Direct-source validation:** Prefer a source link that resolves directly to the official page/document. A search-result snippet or publisher marketing line is a lead, not completed verification.
4. **Cross-check:** Use at least one primary official public source plus one independent expert/resource check where available. If claims conflict, treat the point as unresolved until reconciled.
5. **Sample paper status:** Record whether an item is an official specimen, actual past paper, teacher-created mock, sample response or third-party explanation. These are not interchangeable.
6. **Assessment-change check:** Compare question format, marks, timing, rubric, response length and command terms against the latest verified syllabus/instructions. Older items may be used as skill drills only after item-level comparison with current demands.
7. **Rights and access:** Link to official papers where possible; do not bypass logins or copy protected examination questions, text inserts or model answers into our product. Create original passages and tasks using the validated task architecture.
8. **Uncertainty label:** Use one of “Publicly verified official fact,” “Cross-checked independent interpretation,” “Platform design decision,” or “Unverified / blocked.” Do not convert a teaching recommendation into an official objective code.
9. **Review freshness:** Recheck the official syllabus page and applicable notices at the beginning of each content/revision cycle and before high-stakes release. Store the last checked date.

## 4. Sample-paper policy by curriculum

### IB MYP Language and Literature
MYP is not a single centrally prescribed Year 1 written exam. Official public information says school-based assessments are created and marked by teachers; optional external eAssessment is available, and official specimen examinations are in the password-protected PRC. Therefore:
- Do not build Year 1 around random “MYP past papers” from download sites.
- Use public MYP skill areas and official public programme/assessment descriptions to design original formative and summative tasks.
- Across the year, develop listening, speaking, reading, writing, viewing and presenting; include inquiry, text variety, context, audience, purpose and language choices.
- Create an internal criterion-referenced rubric, labelled **our platform rubric**, not an official IB Year 1 criterion/strand rubric.
- Leave official objective/strand codes and level descriptors unmapped unless a direct public source verifies them. Do not imply the resulting plan guarantees full compliance with current detailed-guide requirements.

### IB DP Language A: Language and Literature
Use the current public course page for course identity and the public 2026 Examiner Instructions for Language A for current assessment technique, including explicit comparison/contrast for Paper 2 criterion B2. Cross-check teaching implications against specialist pages that identify specific exam sessions. The official public IB sample-paper index currently surfaces English A: **Literature** papers, not an equivalent public Language A: Language and Literature specimen pack. Do not label the Literature papers as Language and Literature samples. Use current public model-response discussion only if exact course, exam session and criterion version are recorded; where content is subscription-gated, use the page only as metadata or omit it. Our learner tasks should be original, with fresh text choices and original questions that mirror verified skills rather than reproduce IB exam content.

### Cambridge IGCSE First Language English 0500
For exams 2027–2029, use the official Version 1 syllabus and current public specimen questions/mark schemes. Earlier papers are not assumed to match the revised question order and mark distribution. Cambridge explicitly warns that selected past papers may not reflect the current syllabus. For practice, preserve the current skill and response demand but create original reading extracts and questions rather than republishing protected papers.

## 7. Curriculum-guide rating rule

Use the internal 0–5 quality rubric in the research standard. Separate **research breadth**, **pedagogical/implementation quality** and **current official mapping confidence**; a strong teaching plan cannot compensate for a missing or stale official objective map. A dated official guide may inform a labelled legacy crosswalk, but does not establish currentness. The overall score is an internal design judgement, not an IB/Cambridge alignment score or an independent audit.

## 10. Mandatory pre-selection photograph rights process

**Effective immediately for all new or replacement photographs.** A photograph must not be selected, embedded, committed or shown in a preview until its licence has been checked and recorded. “Free to download,” “royalty-free,” or a stock-site “free” label is not sufficient for the project's copyright-free requirement.

1. **Eligible status:** Use only CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication or an explicit public-domain release by the rights holder. Check the exact image's own source/detail page, not a search result, image aggregator, social repost, or AI-generated summary.
2. **Verify before use:** Confirm and record the exact image page, author/uploader, source URL, explicit CC0/public-domain statement and licence link, date checked, and that the licence applies to that exact file. Add this record before embedding the image in code.
3. **Screen visible content:** Inspect the image itself for identifiable people, children, logos/trademarks, distinctive artwork, private/interior locations, number plates or other third-party material. CC0 clears the creator's copyright to the extent dedicated; it does not automatically clear privacy, publicity, trademark, property or other third-party rights. Prefer images without identifiable people or visible branding. If a separate right could apply and cannot be cleared, reject the image.
4. **Check fit and accessibility:** Verify that the photograph genuinely supports the lesson topic and intended analysis; write accurate alt text and a visible source/credit note even where credit is not legally required.
5. **Record and gate:** Record the file title, creator, source page, licence URL, date checked, reason for selection and visible-content screening. If the page is unavailable, licence is ambiguous, or a rights concern remains unresolved, do not use the image.
6. **Recheck changes:** Every replacement or materially different image requires its own pre-selection check. Keep the source record in the same PR as the asset. A later review does not substitute for pre-selection screening.

A photograph may technically remain copyrighted while its creator dedicates it to CC0. For this workflow, “copyright-free” means a documented CC0/public-domain dedication, not merely a free-to-use stock licence. This process reduces risk but is not a legal guarantee for rights beyond copyright.
