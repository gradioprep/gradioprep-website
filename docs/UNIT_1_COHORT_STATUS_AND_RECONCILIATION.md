## Binding specification applying to all three units

1. **Reading-passage rights:** a platform-original reading passage of 250 words or fewer may remain, including qualifying existing originals. A passage over 250 words must use a recognised writer's verified public-domain work, with exact work/edition/source and intended-territory checks recorded before use. The threshold does not permit copying third-party copyrighted text merely because it is short.
2. **Teacher/editor pack:** every objective item needs its correct answer, question-specific reasoning, and a separate rationale for every alternative; ordering tasks need the full sequence and the logic for each placement. Every typed response needs a question-specific expected range, an ideal target, a model response, evidence/criteria, acceptable interpretations where relevant, and tailored feedback/next steps. AI may draft; an academically responsible human validates.
3. **Live typed-response counter:** every typed-response field shows current words / ideal target and updates on typing, paste, edit, deletion and clear. The target is guidance, never a maximum. No truncation, rejection or `maxLength`. Use the rights-protocol word-count convention and expose the count accessibly.
4. **Images and other assets:** verify the exact file/page licence and intended-use rights before selecting or embedding. A source-level CC0/public-domain declaration does not replace visual/context and territory checks.
5. **Curriculum integrity:** keep each curriculum's objectives and assessment language distinct. Official sources are assessed separately from the route-specific independent Top Five. If exact official mapping is unavailable, label the mapping unverified; this does not block independent-source research or a clearly labelled internal guide. Do not invent official codes or claim endorsement.
