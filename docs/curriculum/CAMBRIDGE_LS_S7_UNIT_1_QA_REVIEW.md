## 2. Academic and learner-suitability findings on v1.2

### Findings that passed
- The course identity and learner-facing claim correctly identify Cambridge Lower Secondary English 0861; the text does not conflate it with English as a Second Language 0876.
- The unit's focused scope—explicit meaning, supported inference, evidence selection and explanation of word choice—is appropriate for a short reading-focused practice unit. It does not claim to assess all four language skills or complete Stage 7 coverage.
- The narrative is internally coherent: the rain gauge is placed in an open paved area; water flow and wind are described consistently; Jamil's decision to help with the seed trays is supported by clues while remaining open to more than one defensible interpretation.
- The answer guide accepts evidence-supported alternatives rather than requiring memorised wording.
- The warm-up and exit check use original short texts; the exit check requires a reasonable inference and two clues.
- The 7Ri.03 crosswalk is limited to the warm-up's direct-statement question, guided practice A and independent practice 1. It is not applied to the whole unit.
- No obvious age-inappropriate, culturally insensitive or distressing content was identified in the short narrative. This is a desk-review observation, not user testing or a formal human safeguarding/accessibility assessment.

### Changes required and applied to v1.3
1. **Missing explicit worked model:** v1.2 moved quickly from identifying an inference to guided practice. A learner could see the answer but not the reasoning steps clearly. v1.3 now models stated clues → inference → why the clues support it using the warm-up example, before learners work with the passage.
2. **Vocabulary access support:** v1.2 left words such as “pane”, “hinge” and “rain gauge” unsupported in the learner file. v1.3 adds an optional word-help box for access words while leaving “skittered” and “crept” unexplained because those are the explicit language-analysis targets.
3. **Annotation access:** v1.2 described underlining, circling and starring without explicitly allowing alternatives. v1.3 permits paper annotation, short notes or S/I labels and states that printing/marking up the passage is not required.
4. **Scoring validity:** v1.2 used one generic 0–2 descriptor across independent items 2–5, although item 2 is a selection-only task and does not require an explanation. v1.3 uses item-specific descriptors: one point for each valid detail on item 2, and distinct inference, language-effect and evaluation descriptors for items 3–5. The formative total remains 15 points and does not claim Cambridge grade equivalence.

## 3. Content-quality and scoring check on v1.3

- The learner-facing model is based only on the warm-up mini-example and does not give away the answers to the main passage questions.
- The optional word help supports access without predefining the two words assessed for language effect.
- The marking descriptors now match the demand of each task. Maximum score: item 1 (1) + items 2–5 (8) + extended response item 6 (6) = **15 points**. The exit task and reflection remain unscored diagnostic activities.
- The stated time estimate of 25–35 minutes remains plausible for the listed timed sections, with additional time permitted for learners who need it.
- The teaching sequence is more consistent with evidence-informed reading instruction: model the reasoning, practise with scaffolding, then transfer to independent tasks.
- The unit remains reading-focused; it should not be presented as a complete Cambridge Stage 7 English course, a Progression Test or a Checkpoint mock.
