## 2. Candidate unit focus

**Unit title:** The Turning Sign  
**Content status:** Draft candidate; not human-approved or released  
**Intended learner group:** Grade 6 English learners  
**Estimated time:** 30–35 minutes, to validate with teachers/learners  
**Text type:** Short, original fable-style narrative written for this platform  
**Internal learning goals (not official CBSE wording):**
- Identify the main problem, key events and resolution in a short narrative.
- Select a relevant detail and explain what it suggests, distinguishing a clue from a proven conclusion.
- Use prepositions of location accurately when describing where an event or object occurs.
- Retell the solution in a clear sequence, using textual evidence and precise location words.

The design is informed by the topic labels and activity headings on the official CIET page. The lesson does not copy the stories, wording, exercises, illustrations or layout of *Poorvi*.

## 3. Learning sequence

1. **Orient — 3 minutes:** Read the short passage once. Establish what has changed about the sign and why that matters.
2. **Track events — 5 minutes:** Identify the problem, the hoofprint clue, the displaced wedge, and the solution.
3. **Model evidence-based inference — 5 minutes:** Show the difference between noticing hoofprints and proving who moved the sign. A relevant clue should guide a search, not become an unsupported accusation.
4. **Guided practice — 8 minutes:** Answer one explicit-information question, one inference question and a sequencing activity.
5. **Language in context — 5 minutes:** Locate the sign and trace the search using *at, near, down, behind,* and *beside*.
6. **Independent transfer — 5–8 minutes:** Write a 50–70 word retelling with a sequence connector, two location words and one supported idea about Lali's change of view.

Timings are planning estimates, not measured learner-performance results.

## 4. Assessment and feedback design

Use short, ungraded formative feedback. Give priority to:
- accuracy of the selected passage detail;
- a clear link between evidence and interpretation;
- event order that matches the narrative;
- correct contextual use of location words;
- a retelling that distinguishes what happened from what a character first guessed.

The teacher/editor pack has a **12-point internal formative guide** for consistency. It is a platform-created classroom aid, not a CBSE rubric, official mark scheme, board prediction or grade conversion.
