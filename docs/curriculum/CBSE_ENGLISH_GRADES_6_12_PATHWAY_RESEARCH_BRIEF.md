## 1. Product decision

Add CBSE English Grades 6–12 as a new pathway family alongside the existing IB and Cambridge English pathways. This is an addition of curriculum scope only. It does **not** change the product architecture, page hierarchy, unit structure, research method, curriculum-rotation pattern, implementation workflow, approval requirements or content-release process.

The binding product architecture remains:

**Pathways catalogue → curriculum/course → class/level → unit → lesson/practice.**

Use the same reusable platform templates and instructional engine as the other tracks, while keeping CBSE's own learning outcomes, texts, skills progression, sequence, assessment formats and official mapping separate. Never copy an IB or Cambridge unit plan and relabel it CBSE.

- **Grades 9–10:** Secondary-stage English. Use the applicable academic-session CBSE curriculum, current NCERT syllabus/textbooks and any relevant changes arising from NCF-SE implementation. Preserve course-choice distinctions such as English Language and Literature and English Communicative wherever each is officially offered/applicable. The pathway label “CBSE English Grades 6–12” is a product-family label, not a claim that CBSE prescribes one identical English course across all seven classes. Exact course destinations must follow the current official class/session evidence.

## 4. Required research and design method

Follow the binding English Curriculum Research Workflow for each grade and applicable route:

1. Identify the current official CBSE/NCERT curriculum, textbook, learning outcomes/competencies, syllabus edition and assessment rules.
2. Trace relevant NCF-SE 2023 principles to grade-specific official sources rather than applying broad principles as if they were exact syllabus requirements.
3. Complete a ranked Top Five review of independent websites separately for the exact CBSE grade and English route. Include relevant India-focused and international specialist teaching/EdTech providers, publishers and educator resources; inspect publicly available grade-band/course-specific material and record dates, access and evidence. Do not count CBSE/NCERT/government sources toward the five. Use the binding weighted rubric in `docs/IB_CAMBRIDGE_CURRICULUM_RESEARCH_WORKFLOW.md` Section 2A.
4. Cross-check proposed pedagogy against reputable evidence and the public-source register. Use platform research to inform implementation quality, not to determine official CBSE requirements.
5. Design a distinct grade-specific curriculum guide and unit sequence, with source traceability and explicit confidence/status for each mapping.
6. Keep official codes/wording only where directly verified. Otherwise use clearly labelled internal skill outcomes and mark the official mapping unresolved.
7. Keep academic/content review, official mapping/currency, rights/originality, accessibility, age/cultural suitability and release approval separate.

## Binding passage-length and teacher-pack standard — updated 30 October 2026

The founder-approved passage threshold applies across CBSE English Grades 6–12:
- Platform-original reading passage bodies up to and including 250 words are permitted; existing qualifying short originals may remain unchanged.
- Passage bodies over 250 words must use recognised writers' verified public-domain works, with exact source/edition and intended-territory checks completed before use.
- The threshold does not permit copying third-party copyrighted text merely because it is short. Photo/documentary-asset rights remain separate.
- Give every passage an Excerpt ID. Short originals receive an authorship/provenance/word-count/suitability record; long public-domain excerpts receive a Source ID and full source record.

Create a separate versioned Evaluation Report for the PA baseline and each completed SA segment, showing the exact class/session/source, item-to-outcome mapping, learner evidence, rubric version, confidence/limitations and teacher-reviewed feedback. Keep the Overall Progress Tracker as a separate cumulative unit/milestone view linking to each report. Reuse existing assessment and analytics records before proposing new schema.


### 14.1 Session 2026–27 route-currency note

The current CBSE Academic curriculum portal is `Curriculum for the Academic Year 2026–27`: [official curriculum](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html). For Class IX, the 2026–27 language scheme allows English at R1 or R2 and identifies the single English course aligned to the new NCERT `Kaveri` textbook. CBSE's [Notification 40/2026, dated 9 April 2026](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2026/40_Notification_2026.pdf), states that English Communicative is discontinued for Class IX from 2026–27; Class X learners who took English Communicative in Class IX may continue it in 2026–27. The current portal separately lists English Core and English Elective for Classes XI–XII. These route changes are version-sensitive: record whether the learner is a new Class IX 2026–27 entrant, a continuing Class X cohort or a senior-secondary English Core/Elective learner before mapping any PA/SA.

Accordingly, do not reuse a legacy Class IX English Communicative PA for the new Class IX 2026–27 route. Class X transition cohorts require their prior Class IX route to be recorded. For Classes XI–XII, map English Core and English Elective separately. Verify the exact class, session, route and official assessment structure before authoring any PA/SA or reporting a skill. This note does not itself authorise any Listening section; the M3/SA3-only and direct-mapping rules remain binding.
