## Why are we learning this?

In this short story, follow the events and notice how a character moves from a quick guess to a better-supported conclusion. Then practise using words that describe location.

## Read the passage

### The Red Marker

Along a forest path, the animals had tied a red marker to a post beside the stream. It showed the safest place to cross after rain. One morning, the marker was gone. Piku the parakeet noticed hoofprints near the post and guessed that the deer had pulled it away. Keshav the tortoise asked him to look more carefully. A narrow strip of red cloth was caught on a thorn under a low branch. It continued across the mud and curled around a fallen log. Behind the log, the marker had wrapped around a bush when a strong gust blew through the trees.

Piku loosened the string and carried the marker back. He retied it higher on the post, then apologised to the deer for his guess. The animals placed a second marker beside the footpath.

“A clue is not the same as proof,” Keshav reminded them. “Look for the whole story before you decide.”

## 1. Find a detail

**Which detail helps the animals discover where the marker has gone?**

- A. The deer had walked near the post.
- B. A strip of red cloth led towards the fallen log.
- C. The marker was used after rain.
- D. The animals placed a second marker beside the footpath.

Write the detail you selected in your own words.

## 2. Explain an inference

Why does Piku apologise to the deer? Use one detail from the passage to support your explanation. Remember that an inference is an interpretation supported by evidence, not just a guess.

## 3. Put events in order

Number these events from 1 to 4.

- The marker is found wrapped around a bush.
- Piku notices hoofprints and guesses what happened.
- Piku apologises to the deer and reties the marker.
- A strip of red cloth is traced past the thorn and fallen log.

## 4. Location words in context

Complete each sentence using a suitable word from this list: **beside · under · across · around · behind**

1. The marker was tied to a post ______ the stream.
2. The red cloth was caught on a thorn ______ a low branch.
3. The strip continued ______ the mud.
4. The cloth curled ______ a fallen log.
5. The marker was found ______ the log.

Use each word once.

## 5. Exit task: retell the solution

Write **50–70 words** explaining how the animals found the marker and what Piku learned. Include:
- at least one event-order word, such as *first, next, then* or *finally*;
- at least two location prepositions from the passage;
- one detail explaining why Piku changed his mind.

There may be more than one reasonable explanation, but it must fit the passage.
