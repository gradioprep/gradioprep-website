# Assessment Curriculum-Mapping and Milestone / Summative Assessment Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 11 November 2026  
**Status:** Founder-directed hard process requirement. Applies to every Preliminary Assessment (PA), milestone assessment and Evaluation Report across the IB, Cambridge and CBSE pathways.  
**Owner:** Academic lead / product owner

## 1. Binding rule — no peripheral assessment content

Every PA and every Summative Assessment (SA) item, question type, task segment and reported skill **must be directly and explicitly mapped** to an integral learning objective, criterion/strand, assessment objective or competency in the learner's exact curriculum route.

This is a release-blocking requirement, not a design preference.

A task must not be included merely because:
- it is broadly associated with English or language learning;
- it appears in a general list of curriculum skills;
- it is useful enrichment, a generic aptitude test or a transferable skill;
- a related skill is taught somewhere in the broader programme;
- a provider or third-party practice resource uses that task type; or
- the item is easy to implement or can be supported by an available asset.

The fact that a curriculum mentions a broad skill area does not by itself require a standalone test section for that skill in every PA or every SA. Assessment is not a checklist of every broad skill label. It must sample the prescribed outcomes that are necessary and appropriate for the specific assessment purpose and segment.

**Unmapped or tangential content is excluded.** If a current official objective/strand cannot be verified, mark the item `UNMAPPED` and keep it out of a scored or diagnostic assessment until the mapping is resolved. Do not invent codes, infer an official mapping from a broad skills list, or relabel a platform taxonomy as an official curriculum code.

## 2. Required mapping record for every assessment item

Before an item can be approved, implemented or included in a report, its record must contain:

1. **Learner route:** board/awarding body, programme, subject/course, grade/class/year/stage, academic session or exam series, and applicable syllabus/specification edition.
2. **Official curriculum source:** exact title, official URL/document identifier, version/date/effective period, date inspected and reviewer.
3. **Exact mapping:** the official criterion and strand, assessment objective, competency or learning outcome. Preserve the source's own identifier and wording where permitted; do not create a pseudo-official code.
4. **Item definition:** task/question type, prompt/stimulus, response mode, expected learner evidence and why that evidence is necessary for the selected outcome.
5. **Mapping strength:** `DIRECT`, `SUPPORTING` or `UNMAPPED`. Only `DIRECT` items may form the PA/SA evidence basis. Supporting evidence may contextualise a directly mapped outcome but must not be presented as an independent tested skill.
6. **Assessment placement:** PA, SA1, SA2 or SA3; milestone/unit set; prerequisite teaching; intended demand and timing.
7. **Review controls:** academic reviewer, date, exact item/content version, accessibility/accommodation effect, rights/source status, scoring method and release state.

Each task segment and each skill claim in an Evaluation Report must be traceable back through this record to the exact official curriculum source. A parent skill label is not sufficient.

## 3. Board- and route-specific authority rules

Do not create one generic English assessment and relabel it for three boards.

### IB

For IB MYP Language and Literature, distinguish the six broad skill areas (listening, speaking, reading, writing, viewing and presenting) from the four subject criteria (A Analysing, B Organising, C Producing text and D Using language) and their strands. The six skill areas are not six mandatory standalone PA sections. Each PA/SA item must map to the relevant criterion/strand and to the exact course/year context.

For MYP Year 4, the Founder-confirmed school placement is Grade 9 / MYP Year 4. The public Language and Literature guide describes the criteria as applying to all programme years but its detailed year-specific descriptors are supplied for Years 1, 3 and 5; do not treat a Year 3 or Year 5 descriptor as an exact Year 4 descriptor. Obtain/verify the current authorised Year 4 progression with the school/IB materials. Until then, record exact Year 4 mapping as pending human academic confirmation and do not claim the PA has been officially calibrated to Year 4. This standard does not claim that GradioPrep tasks are official IB assessments or that its internal profiles convert to IB grades.

For IB DP Language A: Language and Literature, use the current DP course guide and the applicable assessment component/criterion requirements. Do not borrow MYP criteria or task forms merely because the subject family has a similar name.

### Cambridge

Use the exact qualification, syllabus code, exam series, component and candidate route. For Cambridge IGCSE First Language English 0500, distinguish AO1 Reading, AO2 Writing and AO3 Speaking and Listening, and verify the candidate's route. Component 4 Speaking and Listening is optional and separately endorsed; do not insert an AO3 Listening assessment for a candidate whose selected route does not include it. Cambridge Lower Secondary English 0861 and IGCSE 0500 are separate courses and must have separate objective maps.

### CBSE

Use the exact class, academic session, subject/course option, prescribed curriculum/textbook and current official competencies or assessment structure. The CBSE English label across Classes 6–12 is a product family, not one uniform syllabus. Preserve route distinctions (including applicable secondary/senior-secondary course variants and current session changes). Do not copy an outcome from one class or English option into another class's PA without direct evidence.

The latest official source and academic-session currency must be checked for the exact learner route. If only broad framework evidence exists, it may inform teaching design but does not establish an exact assessment mapping.

## 4. PA and milestone assessment sequence

The following is GradioPrep's product progression model. The labels **PA**, **Milestone 1–3**, and **SA1–SA3** are platform terms; do not claim that IB, Cambridge or CBSE uses these exact names unless the respective board does so.

- **Preliminary Assessment (PA):** the entry diagnostic before the first milestone. It establishes a baseline using only directly mapped, age/level-appropriate question types and skills needed for the learner's exact course. It must not assess future milestone content that has not been taught.
- **Milestone 1:** the defined set of curriculum units assigned to the first progress segment. **SA1** is the summative assessment for that completed unit set. SA1 assesses only the directly mapped outcomes taught in those units and the stated prerequisite outcomes needed to interpret them. No Listening task is included in SA1.
- **Milestone 2:** the defined set of curriculum units assigned to the second progress segment. **SA2** is the summative assessment for that completed unit set. SA2 assesses only directly mapped outcomes taught in those units and relevant retained prerequisites. No Listening task is included in SA2.
- **Milestone 3:** the defined set of curriculum units assigned to the third progress segment. **SA3** is the summative assessment for that completed unit set. Listening is reserved for this stage, never for the PA, SA1 or SA2. It is included in SA3 only where the exact course/session/route has a directly mapped Listening outcome and the task is necessary for that route. The asset, item, access arrangements, scoring and timing still require human academic approval.

A milestone is the **set of units completed in that segment**; it is not a single test. An SA is the assessment of the unit set for its corresponding milestone. The segment's report records what the learner demonstrated against the mapped outcomes in that segment.

If the unit set or relevant official mapping changes, version the milestone/SA definition and review all affected tasks before use. Do not silently move a task or reuse a prior SA as though it assessed a different unit set.

## 5. Listening placement rule

Listening is **excluded from the Preliminary Assessment, SA1 and SA2**. This remains true even if listening is a broad skill area in the board's overall language curriculum.

Listening is reserved for **Milestone 3 / SA3**. Before it is activated, the academic team must:
- identify the exact M3 units and the official Listening outcome(s) taught in them;
- verify the exact board/course/class/year/session/component route;
- map each Listening question to that outcome and the evidence the learner must demonstrate;
- establish that a standalone listening item is appropriate and not a tangential test;
- approve the stimulus rights/source, intelligibility, language variety, pace, accessibility and playback/replay policy;
- validate timing, scoring/accepted responses and teacher review; and
- ensure that the SA3 report shows the actual mapped outcome and the learner evidence.

The previous VOA source and L01–L05 question draft is a **deferred SA3 candidate only**. It is not approved PA content and is not automatically approved for SA3. It must be re-evaluated against M3 unit content and the applicable official course route before use. A licence solves only the asset-rights question; it does not prove curriculum alignment or assessment placement.

## 6. Evaluation Reports and analytics

The analytics experience must keep two distinct views:

### Segment Evaluation Report

After each completed assessment, create a separate, versioned Evaluation Report for the corresponding scope:
- PA baseline report (entry evidence only);
- Milestone 1 / SA1 segment report;
- Milestone 2 / SA2 segment report; and
- Milestone 3 / SA3 segment report.

Each report must show the milestone/unit set or PA baseline scope; the board/course/session and exact curriculum source/version; the criterion/strand/AO/competency mapped to each assessed item; whether the evidence is direct or supporting; the learner's answer/performance evidence; the approved scoring/rubric version; confidence/insufficient-evidence limitations; teacher-reviewed feedback and next learning actions. A report must not claim a skill was tested when it was only indirectly observed or not assessed.

Reports are immutable/versioned snapshots of the content, mapping and assessment version actually used. A changed mapping or revised SA creates a new version; it must not rewrite a report for an earlier attempt.

### Overall Progress Tracker

The Overall Progress Tracker is a separate longitudinal view. It shows cumulative progress through Milestones 1–3 and the units within them, with links to each segment's separate Evaluation Report. It is not a substitute for the report and must not collapse three segment assessments into an unsupported single board grade. Any cumulative summary must identify its method, evidence scope and limitations.

### 6.1 Current record-reuse finding — 11 November 2026

The read-only physical-record audit is recorded in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md` §6.23. Existing versioned rubric, task-level assessment/submission, criterion-result, published-feedback and private analytics records are reusable as evidence inputs. The inspected migrations do not provide persisted curriculum-unit membership/progression or composite segment Evaluation Report snapshots. Do not treat the static frontend pathway slots as learner progression records. Before adding schema, lock exact board/course/session/syllabus routes and approved unit sets; then add only the missing versioned milestone/unit-set and report relations. Preserve separate PA/SA segment reports and the longitudinal Overall Progress Tracker.

## 7. Release gates and enforcement

An assessment cannot be marked ready until:
- every item has a `DIRECT` route-specific mapping;
- every required segment maps only to content taught by that milestone;
- no peripheral, tangential or generic skill-test item remains;
- Listening appears only in M3/SA3 and only for a route where it is directly mapped;
- the Evaluation Report can display the same item-to-curriculum mapping used by the assessment;
- accessibility, rights, age/level suitability and human academic review pass; and
- synthetic regression tests verify the correct assessment version, milestone/unit set, item mapping and route restrictions.

When a mapping cannot be verified, the correct action is to remove/defer the item, not to broaden its label. This gate applies to all PAs and all SAs for the IB, Cambridge and CBSE pathway families and their individual courses/routes.
